{{paragraph}}

Return one field, marked: the paragraph word for word, with " / " placed between thought groups.

- A thought group is a stretch a native reader says in one breath with one main stress: a subject with its modifiers, a verb with its objects, a prepositional phrase, a clause after a comma or conjunction.
- Put " / " only between groups. Never inside a group: not between an article and its noun, a preposition and its object, an auxiliary and its verb, or a name and its title.
- Every comma, period, semicolon and colon ends a group. Do not mark a sentence boundary twice: the punctuation already shows it.
- Groups are three to eight words. A long sentence gets several groups; a short sentence may be one.
- Change nothing else: same words, same order, same punctuation, no line breaks, no markdown.
